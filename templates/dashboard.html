Questions

Frequently asked

Is Solo just another to-do app?
+

No. Solo combines task management, AI-powered day planning, and a contextual AI assistant into one system. The difference is that Solo knows your full context: your tasks, your plan, your history, and uses it to help you make better decisions, not just check boxes.

How does the AI actually help me?
+

The AI has access to all your open tasks, today's schedule, and your conversation history. You can ask it what to focus on, tell it to add or update tasks, get a summary of what is overdue, or just think out loud. It responds with specific, useful answers, not generic productivity advice.

What happens when I want to grow my team?
+

Solo has a built-in team handoff feature. When you invite your first team member, the AI automatically generates a personalised onboarding brief explaining what you are building, your current priorities, and how you work. They hit the ground running before day one.

Is my data private?
+

Yes. Your tasks, plans, and conversations are stored in your own database. Solo does not sell your data or use it for advertising. The AI calls are made to OpenAI using your own API key, so you stay in full control.

Do I need to pay for anything?
+

Solo itself is free and open source. The only cost is your OpenAI API key for the AI features, which costs a few cents per day for normal use. You own and run the app yourself.
